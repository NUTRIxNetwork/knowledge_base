## Programme

!!! note "Draft programme"
    Speakers and titles are being confirmed. This outline is indicative.

### Day 1 — Predictive modelling of multi-modal nutrition data

- Member talks on predictive modelling of multi-modal nutrition data and on the challenges of ML in postprandial studies
- Expert talk on machine-learning methods and theory
- External talk on methods for metabolomics and multi-omics data
- Member talk on clinical validation, from theory to practice

### Day 2 — Open science, reproducibility, and making nutrition data FAIR

- Keynote on trustworthy system design: robustness, explainability, fairness
- Expert talks on modern machine-learning approaches for massive biomedical data, and on ML for host–bacteria interactions
- Hands-on workshop — *Avoiding common pitfalls: hands-on ML in Python with real nutrition datasets*: stability and uncertainty versus sample size, repeating validation procedures, feature-selection stability, and the sensitivity of feature importance to the choice of metric

### Day 3 — Network working day

Reserved for network members.
