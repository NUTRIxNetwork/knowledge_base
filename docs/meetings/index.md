# Meetings

Each year the NUTRIx network hosts an **in-person meeting** built around one of
its three core themes. Every meeting combines keynote lectures, member talks,
expert presentations, and hands-on workshops.

!!! success "Days 1–2 are open to the community"
    The first two days of each in-person meeting are **open to interested
    researchers** from the wider nutrition and data science community. Keynotes,
    expert talks, and workshop walkthroughs are recorded with speaker consent and
    published as open-access tutorials in this knowledge base.

## 2027 · Reproducible and Transparent Machine Learning and AI in Nutrition Research

*Joachim Herz House, Atlanta, USA · host coordinator: Prof. Tilman Kühn*

Held together with trainees from the **NIH Precision Nutrition Training
Programme** for transatlantic exchange and alignment of methodological standards.
Focus: reproducible ML workflows, FAIR data practices, and explainable AI.

**Day 1 — Predictive modelling of multi-modal nutrition data**

- Member talks on predictive modelling of multi-modal nutrition data and on the challenges of ML in postprandial studies
- Expert talk on machine-learning methods and theory
- External talk on methods for metabolomics and multi-omics data
- Member talk on clinical validation, from theory to practice

**Day 2 — Open science, reproducibility, and making nutrition data FAIR**

- Keynote on trustworthy system design: robustness, explainability, fairness
- Expert talks on modern machine-learning approaches for massive biomedical data, and on ML for host–bacteria interactions
- Hands-on workshop — *Avoiding common pitfalls: hands-on ML in Python with real nutrition datasets*: stability and uncertainty versus sample size, repeating validation procedures, feature-selection stability, and the sensitivity of feature importance to the choice of metric

## 2028 · Dietary Data, Disease Risk, and the Individual

*German Institute of Human Nutrition Potsdam-Rehbrücke (DIfE), Nuthetal · host coordinator: Dr. Fabian Eichelmann*

Focus: operationalising dietary intake data, survival analysis in nutritional
epidemiology, and N-of-1 trials for individual-level evidence (including wearable
data).

**Day 1 — From plate to dataset to research question**

- Member talk on dietary intake data and procedures
- Expert talks on smartphone-based dietary assessment, and on generating dietary patterns to study diet and disease
- Hands-on workshop — *Working with large-scale nutritional data at population level*: deriving intake variables (nutrients, foods, patterns), intake distributions and nutrient adequacy in populations and subgroups, and energy-adjustment strategies

**Day 2 — Connecting diet with disease risk**

- Member talks on confounder-adjustment strategies with DAGs, and on survival outcomes with dietary data (prediction versus inference, substitution models)
- Hands-on workshop on survival analysis in cohort data (EPIC-Potsdam synthetic dataset), DAG-informed adjustment, and substitution and non-linear models with restricted cubic splines
- N-of-1 trials for precision nutrition, and a hands-on session designing and running a mini N-of-1 trial in [StudyU](https://studyu.health) — through to "N-of-1 to N-of-many"

## 2029 · Multi-omics Data Integration in Nutrition

*Kiel University · host coordinator: Prof. Helena U. Zacharias*

Focus: integrating multimodal datasets — microbiome, metabolomics, and dietary
intake — through harmonisation, dimensionality reduction, and multi-omics
modelling.

**Day 1 — Multimodal datasets, common challenges, and dimensionality-reduction and feature-selection strategies**

- Expert talk on metabolic modelling of diet and the gut microbiome
- Member talks on data-integration challenges and pitfalls, postprandial resilience and metabolic phenotypes, microbiome-sequencing data formats and standards, and responder analysis in nutritional interventions
- Hands-on workshop — *Data processing, harmonisation & feature selection*: missing values and batch effects, dimensionality reduction, and feature selection (LASSO, random-forest importance)

**Day 2 — End-to-end omics pipelines and integration strategies**

- Keynote on real-world multimodal data integration in nutrition research
- Member talks on integrating microbiome, clinical, and dietary data, and on LC-MS and NMR data characteristics
- Hands-on workshop — *Microbiome & metabolomics integration*: multivariable modelling with association testing and integration frameworks (MaAsLin3-style models, multiblock PLS, DIABLO, MOFA), co-occurrence and correlation networks, and pathway enrichment and biological interpretation
