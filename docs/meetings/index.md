# Meetings

Each year the NUTRIx network hosts an **in-person meeting** built around one of
its three core themes, combining keynote lectures, expert talks, and hands-on
workshops.

!!! success "Days 1–2 are open to the community"
    The first two days of each in-person meeting are **open to interested
    researchers** from the wider nutrition and data science community. Keynotes,
    expert talks, and workshop walkthroughs are recorded with speaker consent and
    published as open-access tutorials in this knowledge base.

## The three meetings

| Year | Theme | Location |
|------|-------|----------|
| **2027** | [Reproducible and transparent machine learning and AI in nutrition research](2027-atlanta.md) | Joachim Herz House, Atlanta, USA |
| **2028** | [Dietary data, disease risk, and the individual](2028-nuthetal.md) | DIfE, Nuthetal, Germany |
| **2029** | [Multi-omics data integration in nutrition](2029-kiel.md) | Kiel University, Germany |
